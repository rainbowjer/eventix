EventiX: Digital E-Ticketing System
====================================

In recent years, the rise of digital ticketing systems has transformed how events and services handle ticket sales and attendance tracking. Electronic Ticketing (e-ticketing), a paperless solution that allows users to book, manage, and validate tickets online, has gained popularity due to its convenience, security, and efficiency. According to Jeyakarthika et al. (2022), e-ticketing systems enhance customer experience by removing the need for physical tickets, lowering the risk of forgery, and enabling quick entry to events or services. Furthermore, the environmental advantages of e-ticketing, which reduce paper consumption, align with global initiatives aimed at promoting sustainable practices across various industries (Singh & Pandey, 2023).

This project, titled EventiX, aims to create an e-ticketing system that provides a user-friendly platform for purchasing and managing tickets digitally for the event and management industry. By incorporating features such as QR code verification and real-time booking updates, this system will optimize ticketing operations for event organizers and service providers while improving the user experience. Additionally, the project utilizes secure database management to safeguard user data, addressing concerns related to data privacy and digital security in online transactions (Zhang & Wei, 2021).
